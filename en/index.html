About

For twelve years I worked in IKEA's digital channels, most recently as Group Product Manager at Inter IKEA, owning Sales & Customer Payment for the IKEA franchisees: cart and checkout, payments, the retail app, the coworker sales tool and store checkout.

How I lead: servant leadership and iteration. I give teams context and mandate, clear obstacles, test early with real users and treat plans as hypotheses.

Before product management I was a lawyer, and then moved into ERP and e-commerce consulting. That mix still shapes how I work: structured, at home with contracts and regulation, and focused on what creates value.

Based in Lund, working across the Öresund region.
